Free Caregiver Planning Checklist: Benefits, Documents & Support

Caregivers often coordinate healthcare, household needs, benefits and important paperwork at once. This checklist helps families start with official free resources while keeping sensitive information private.

1. Discuss care preferences

Ask the person receiving care which treatments, settings and people matter most to them. Record their preferences using your state's official advance directive instructions where possible.

State advance directive forms ↗

2. Identify decision-makers

Discuss healthcare and financial powers of attorney. The chosen people should understand the role, consent to it and be reachable. Different states have different signing requirements.

Find free legal-aid help ↗

3. Locate local services

Use the federal Eldercare Locator to find Area Agencies on Aging, meal assistance, transportation, caregiver support and other services. A family member can ask for help even before a crisis.

Eldercare Locator ↗

4. Check respite and disability services

Ask local services about respite care, home modifications, transportation, and disability resources. Availability and eligibility vary by locality and funding.

Federal community living resources ↗

5. Understand hospice and palliative care

These services serve different needs. A clinician can explain when hospice is appropriate and what Medicare covers; palliative care can be provided at other stages of illness.

Medicare hospice coverage ↗

6. Keep a private document checklist

Store a list of healthcare agents, emergency contacts, medications, providers, policies, directives and the location of the original legal documents. Avoid uploading confidential details to public websites.
