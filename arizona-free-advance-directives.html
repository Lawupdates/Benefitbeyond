Free Arizona Living Will & Healthcare Power of Attorney Forms (2026)

Arizona residents can download a no-cost Life Care Planning Packet from the Arizona Attorney General. It includes important healthcare decision forms and directions for completing them.

Where to get the official packet

The Arizona Attorney General distributes a complete Life Care Planning Packet free of charge. You can download it, request mailed copies, or visit an office. It includes a healthcare power of attorney, mental healthcare power of attorney, living will, and prehospital medical directive.

Arizona Attorney General packet ↗

How to complete it correctly

Read the instructions in the packet before signing. Some documents have witness or notarization rules. The prehospital DNR must use the required orange format. It is a medical order, not a replacement for a living will.

Arizona courts instructions ↗

Register and share copies

Arizona has a Healthcare Directives Registry. Registration can help medical providers access directives in an emergency, but simply completing a form does not automatically register it. Tell your healthcare agent where to find copies.

Registry information ↗

Is a will or trust included?

No. These free medical planning forms are not a last will and testament or a living trust. Those documents govern different decisions, including assets and distributions. Contact nonprofit legal aid for questions about estate documents.
