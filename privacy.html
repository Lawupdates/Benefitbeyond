Privacy & Your Data

Genie of Benefits™ is an independent informational website. You do not need to create an account to explore its current public resources.

Information you choose to save

On the current static website, saved opportunity ideas, grocery lists, and display preferences may be stored in your own browser's local storage. They can be lost if you clear browser data, use a private window, or change devices. They are not synchronized with a personal account.

Privacy-conscious visitor counting

We record an approximate daily visitor count to understand how many people use our site. A browser is counted at most once per Phoenix calendar day using a short-lived, essential counting cookie. Our private counter stores a randomly named count record—not a visitor name, account, IP address, or individual browsing history. Automated bot traffic and browser Do Not Track requests are excluded where detectable. Only the owner can view the totals through a password-protected server-side dashboard. This counter begins when deployed, so earlier traffic is not included. Hosting and network providers may separately process technical logs as described below.

Hosting and technical information

Our hosting provider and ordinary internet infrastructure may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver the site and protect its systems. We do not claim that browsing is anonymous. The site does not currently offer a logged-in account for benefits applications.

External websites

Resource links can send you to government agencies, healthcare directories, or other external websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may collect sensitive information. Do not enter Social Security numbers, Medicare numbers, passwords, or medical records into an unverified form.

Advertising

Sponsored content, if introduced, must be visibly identified. Advertising partners do not receive any user benefits application from this website because we do not process such applications. Future analytics, advertising scripts, or forms will require a corresponding update to this policy before use.

Your choices

Use the accessibility controls to reduce motion. You can erase locally saved ideas via your browser's site data controls. For general site and sponsorship information, see our advertiser information page.
