A free 7-step benefits discovery checklist

  1. Write down which benefit programs already cover your household, if any.
  2. Check federal and state help with prescriptions, food, and healthcare premiums.
  3. Look into utility, housing, telephone and internet assistance programs.
  4. Review community organizations and Area Agencies on Aging for local help.
  5. Explore legal-aid resources and advance directive forms before a crisis.
  6. Save official provider links and check eligibility before applying.
  7. Recheck programs when income, household size, age or health coverage changes.
