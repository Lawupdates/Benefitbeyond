Measurement & campaign transparency

No fabricated audience numbers. Verified monthly users, impressions, clicks, referral conversions and audience breakdowns are not yet published. Request a current traffic report before making a purchase decision. We do not promise traffic, sales, leads or results.

Before launching, sponsor and publisher should agree in writing on the creative, placement URL, labeling, start/end dates, total price, tracking method, reporting cadence, refunds/cancellation terms and approval process. Personal or medical data must not be disclosed for ad targeting.

Editorial and sponsorship standards

  • Paid content is prominently identified as Advertisement or Sponsored.
  • Sponsorship does not influence eligibility advice, independent public-benefit listings or editorial rankings.
  • No misleading government affiliations, guaranteed benefit approvals, predatory lending, deceptive health claims or sensitive-data targeting.
  • Creative should be accessible, mobile-friendly and respectful; no autoplay audio or flashing animations.
  • We may reject or remove a campaign that fails safety, accuracy, legality or editorial standards.
