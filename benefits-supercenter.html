🛡️ Protect yourself from benefit scams

  • Use official government and nonprofit sites whenever possible.
  • Never share a Social Security number or banking password with a discount directory.
  • Check whether a purported government grant or benefit requires a suspicious advance fee.
  • Report impersonation and fraud through ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
