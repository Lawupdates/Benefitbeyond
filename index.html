💡 Quick answers, without the jargon

Does SSDI automatically qualify me for discounts?

No. Some programs use income, age, disability documentation, Medicaid, Medicare or other eligibility rules.

Can I work while receiving SSDI?

Possibly. Trial work periods and other work incentives may apply. Check the official Social Security work rules before relying on an earnings amount.

Can I get help with prescription costs?

Medicare Extra Help and Medicare Savings Programs may help eligible people. See the official links above.
